Data deletion request

Arrow Out · Effective 12 November 2026

Arrow Out does not require an account, and the developer runs no server. Almost all data stays on your device, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress, stars, coins, daily-challenge state, and settings are stored only in the app's local storage. Delete them by any of these methods:

  • In the app: Settings → Reset progress, then Yes, reset everything.
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Arrow Out → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstall the app.

The developer holds no copy of this data and cannot read or recover it.

Advertising data

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google receives device identifiers (including your Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interactions. The developer does not receive or store this data. To delete or reset it:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads — delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalised advertising.
  • In the app: Settings → Ad privacy (where shown) — change or withdraw your consent.
  • Google: Ad Settings and Google's Privacy Policy.

Request deletion by email

If you want help with a deletion request, email jcradyn@gmail.com with the subject “Arrow Out data deletion” and describe what you want deleted. Do not send passwords. We will respond within 30 days. Because the developer holds no game data, most requests resolve to the steps above; anything held by Google is covered by Google's own deletion tools.

Arrow Out collects no name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location, and has no user accounts.
