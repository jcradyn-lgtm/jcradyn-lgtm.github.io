Privacy Policy — Arrow Out

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Arrow Out is an ad-supported puzzle game published by Your Studio ("we", "us"). This page explains what the app stores, what advertising partners collect, and the choices you have. It applies to the Arrow Out app on Google Play.

Short version: we do not ask for an account and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. Game progress stays on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses device and advertising data as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game progress locally using your browser/WebView storage: levels completed, stars, coins, hint tokens, daily-challenge streaks, and your sound, haptics and settings preferences. This data never leaves your device through the app, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. We have no server and cannot read it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions — impressions, clicks and approximate view time
  • Diagnostic and crash data related to ad delivery

This data is used to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, measure performance, prevent fraud, and — where you have consented — personalize advertising. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your choices

  • Where required by law (including the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before serving personalized ads, and you can change or withdraw that choice from the consent prompt or the app's settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Google → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Declining one never blocks game progress.
  • You can request information about, or deletion of, data associated with you by contacting us at the address below. Data held by Google is subject to Google's own request tools.

Children

Arrow Out is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are not configured for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information and we do not share data with anyone other than the advertising provider described above. Game progress is retained on your device until you delete it. Ad-related data is retained by Google according to its policies.

Security

The app transmits ad requests over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we keep the app's data collection to the minimum needed to show ads and save progress.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example, if a new SDK or feature is added — we will update this page and the "last updated" date before the change takes effect. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
